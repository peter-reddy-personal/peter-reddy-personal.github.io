Zwift Ladder challenger comparison

What this tool does

Select your team to compare it with opponents within 15 ranking places. Comparisons use the SPR, PUN, CLI, PUR, and END vELO2 factors from ZwiftRacing.app.

Team rosters and rankings are taken from the cached Club Ladder data and rider performance data is loaded from ZwiftRacing.app.
