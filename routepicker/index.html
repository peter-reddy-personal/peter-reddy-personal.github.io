Zwift Ladder route picker

What this tool does

The Zwift Ladder Route Picker helps choose the best race route based on the strengths of your team and the potential riders racing against you. Enter your opponents, select/unselect riders, and the tool uses ZwiftRacing.app vELO2 scores to analyse every Zwift route and show where your team has the biggest advantage.

Team rosters are taken from a cached version of the Club Ladder site, and may miss recently added or removed riders (within the last few days).

vELO2 (current) and power (90d best) data is taken from ZwiftRacing.app.

vELO2 explained ▼

vELO2 scores every rider and route across six performance factors: sprint, punch, climb, TT, pursuit, and endurance. The route vELO weighting is split ~50% between sprint, punch and climb based on the finish profile of the route, and ~50% between pursuit and endurance based on its length/overall difficulty. TT is not used in team racing.

A flat finish such as Glasgow Crit Circuit emphasises sprint and punch, while a 5 minute wall finish like Petite Douleur is entirely weighted toward climbing. Pursuit and endurance measure how well a rider survives the demands of shorter and longer races. Here is a list of all the route weightings.

A rider’s vELO2 score for a route is calculated by multiplying their individual factor strengths by the importance (weight) of each factor for that route. The tool ranks routes by the average vELO2 advantage between the two teams, selecting the routes where each team has the strongest statistical edge.
